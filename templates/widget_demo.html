Pāṇḍitya on your site

Any website can offer Pāṇḍitya to its readers with one line of HTML. It adds a small Pāṇḍitya button in the corner of the page (there's one on this page). The button opens a search and a graph over the page, without leaving it:

  • Small: search for an author or work and see its direct connections.
  • Expanded: the full Pāṇḍitya, with every feature, over the page.
  • Open at panditya.info: the same graph in a new tab.

Install

Add this anywhere in your page:

<script src="https://panditya.info/widget.js" async></script>

Point to authors and works

Mark names with their Pandit ID, and a click opens the widget on them. Try it: the Nyāyasāra of Bhāsarvajña, Nyāyasāra; the author Abhinavagupta; or a search for Kālidāsa. 

<span data-panditya-work="89000">Nyāyasāra</span>
<span data-panditya-author="85352">Abhinavagupta</span>
<span data-panditya-search="Kālidāsa">Kālidāsa</span>

Pages that already link to Pandit records can have those links open the widget instead, by adding data-pandit-links="true" to the script tag. Ctrl- or Cmd-click still follows the link. This page does that: Nyāyasāra on Pandit.

Options

Set these on the script tag:

  • data-position="left": the button sits bottom left instead of bottom right.
  • data-launcher="false": no button; the widget opens only from marked names, Pandit links or script.
  • data-pandit-links="true": links to Pandit records open the widget.

From your own script:

Panditya.open({ works: '89000' })        // or authors, ids (either kind), search
Panditya.open({ authors: '85352', size: 'large' })
Panditya.expand(); Panditya.shrink(); Panditya.close();
